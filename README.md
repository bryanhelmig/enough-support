# Enough · App support

Enough shows how your recorded exercise adds up over the last seven days.

### How do I record a workout?

Wear a compatible Bluetooth heart-rate strap, allow Bluetooth access, then start an open workout or an interval session. The workout clock starts when the first heart-rate reading arrives.

### How do I include Apple Health workouts?

In Enough’s Settings, turn on **Include Apple Health workouts** and allow read access to Workouts and Heart Rate. A workout needs usable heart-rate samples to earn intensity credit.

### Get in touch

[Open a support request](https://github.com/bryanhelmig/enough-support/issues/new) for help with Enough. This is a public issue tracker. Include your iOS version and what you expected to happen. Please leave health records, heart-rate readings and other personal information out of your request.
