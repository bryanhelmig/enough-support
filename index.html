Enough

App support

Enough shows how your recorded exercise adds up over the last seven days.

Record a workout

Wear a compatible Bluetooth heart-rate strap, allow Bluetooth access, then start an open workout or an interval session. The workout clock starts when the first heart-rate reading arrives.

Include Apple Health workouts

In Enough’s Settings, turn on “Include Apple Health workouts” and allow read access to Workouts and Heart Rate. A workout needs heart-rate samples to earn intensity credit.

Get in touch

Open a support request for help with Enough. This is a public issue tracker. Include your iOS version and what you expected to happen. Please leave out health records, heart-rate readings and other personal information.
