Enough

Privacy policy

Effective November 3, 2026.

Enough does not collect any data. The developer cannot see your workouts, heart rate, or anything else you do in the app.

What stays on your iPhone

Workouts you record, including heart-rate readings from a Bluetooth strap.

Your profile: age, resting heart rate, and maximum heart rate if you enter one.

Settings such as appearance and interval cues.

These are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes its local records. Workouts in Apple Health are managed separately.

Apple Health

If you turn on "Include Apple Health workouts", Enough asks for read access to Workouts and Heart Rate. It reads them on your iPhone to estimate exercise minutes. It never writes to Apple Health, never copies Health data into its own storage, and never sends Health data anywhere. You can turn access off at any time in the Health app.

Bluetooth

Enough connects to standard Bluetooth heart-rate straps to read your heart rate during a workout. It does not use Bluetooth for anything else.

What Enough does not do

No account or sign-in.

No ads.

No analytics or tracking.

No server. Enough does not upload any data.

Contact

For help, visit Enough support. Please keep personal health information out of public support requests.
